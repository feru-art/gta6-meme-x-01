A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty: a Vice City sunset scene where a fan who waited 13 years for GTA 6 stands beside his sports car while make.fun's ghost mascot, holding its magenta star coin, floats next to him. The caption reads "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6 / MY AI AGENT MADE $100 WHILE I WAITED". The meme was posted on X from a public account, and this repository hands the grader the post's address: `POST.json` names the post, and `main.ts` copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
